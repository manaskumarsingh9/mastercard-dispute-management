### The New "Strategist" Workflow

1.  **Phase 1: Ingestion (Instant)**
    * **Agent 1 (Summarizer)** creates the "Issuer Summary" so the dashboard is never empty.
2.  **Phase 2: The Strategy & Enrichment (User-Triggered)**
    * **Agent 2 (Strategist) - Task 1:** Reads the claim and identifies "Weird Details." It creates a **Custom Fetch Plan** (e.g., "I need a warehouse photo because the customer claims the box arrived unsealed").
    * **Evidence Engine:** Executes the plan across your 500+ adapters.
    * **Agent 2 (Strategist) - Task 2:** Verifies the results. It creates an **Annotated Evidence Map** highlighting what was found (✅) and what is missing (❌).
3.  **Phase 3: The Human Bridge (Optional)**
    * **Manual Upload:** The user sees the ❌ gaps and can manually upload a missing document to "complete" the map.
4.  **Phase 4: The Final Recommendation & Build**
    * **Agent 4 (Advisor):** Reviews the *final* combined evidence (Auto + Manual) and gives the **Win-Probability**.
    * **Agent 3b (Writer):** Drafts the final rebuttal.
